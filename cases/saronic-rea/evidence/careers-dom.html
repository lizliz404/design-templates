Across disciplines, your work advances a mission to deter conflict, save lives and keep people out of harm’s way. You will be trusted to move quickly, work across teams and own outcomes that matter. 01The Work

Build it here. Prove it at sea.

Your work moves quickly from CAD and code to fabrication, integration and open-water testing. Our vessels evolve as the demands and threats facing them evolve. You will see what holds up, what breaks and deliver what the mission demands next.

Inside Saronic
“I love the challenge, and I’m never bored. I’m always doing something, always learning something new.”

Katja came to Saronic looking for more rigor and more opportunities to learn. On any given day she might run fatigue calculations, build in CAD, meet with software and perception teams, or head to the shop floor to see the physical product take shape and eventually operate in the field. Her curiosity extends beyond engineering: one new challenge each year, from improv and ballet to Python and a screenplay.

Mission Proven

Autonomy that shows up when lives are on the line.

When pilots went down in the Strait of Hormuz, Corsair executed the first known at-sea rescue by an autonomous vessel. That capability was built on over 100,000 nm and more than 20,000 hours of testing to ensure our vessels can deliver when they are needed most. That is what is at stake: extending reach, keeping people out of harm’s way and helping bring them home.
