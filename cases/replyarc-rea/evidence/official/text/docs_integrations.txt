Integration setup 
ReplyArc connects to the tools you already use. Most connections are one-click OAuth; tokens are encrypted at rest and never shown again after connecting. 
All docs 

Messaging channels 
Settings → Channels . Connect WhatsApp Business and Messenger so your agent answers on those channels. Paste the provider access token and (for WhatsApp) the phone-number id; ReplyArc stores it encrypted and the channel adapters send and receive on your behalf. 
CRM & commerce 
Settings → Integrations → CRM & commerce. Click Connect to run OAuth: 
HubSpot / Salesforce : live. Contacts the agent captures push into your CRM hourly (one-way, contacts with an email address). Sync runs show under Settings → Integrations. 
Shopify : coming soon. Today, import your catalog as CSV under Catalog and the agent recommends from it in chat. 
Zendesk : coming soon. Ticket and help-center reads are on the roadmap; ReplyArc's own ticketing is live under Tickets . 
Automations 
Zapier , Make and n8n are live: paste your catch-hook / inbound-webhook URL on the Integrations card and every event ReplyArc emits (conversations, messages, tickets, CRM contacts and deals) POSTs there with HMAC-SHA256 signatures and automatic retries, starting with a test payload on connect. For finer control, register per-event endpoints under Settings → Webhooks . Notification routing to Slack / Teams is configured under Settings → Notifications. 
Calendars & payments 
Google Calendar / Outlook / Calendly / Cal.com : coming soon. Today, bookings run through the widget's booking shortcut, which sends visitors to your booking page in one tap. 
PayPal : coming soon. 
Marketing & analytics 
Mailchimp : coming soon. 
Google Analytics (GA4) : coming soon. The widget already reports funnel events to your own GA4 tag today (see Analytics docs). 
Segment : coming soon. 
Knowledge sources 
Knowledge comes from website crawls (with scheduled re-crawls), file uploads ( PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV ) and articles you author in the dashboard. Importing from Notion, Google Drive, Confluence and OneDrive is on the roadmap and not available yet. 
Disconnecting 
Every connection has a Disconnect action that deletes the stored token. To rotate, disconnect and reconnect.
