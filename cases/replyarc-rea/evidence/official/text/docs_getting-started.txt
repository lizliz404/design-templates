Getting started 
From sign-up to a live, knowledgeable agent on your site in a few steps. 
All docs 

1. Sign up & onboarding 
Create an account, then the onboarding wizard walks you through naming your workspace, creating your first agent, and adding initial knowledge. You can revisit any step later from the dashboard. 
2. Set up your agent 
Each workspace runs a single AI agent. Onboarding creates it for you; open Agents to manage its name, system prompt (its persona and rules) and model defaults. If your workspace doesn't have an agent yet, you'll find a New agent button there. All knowledge, settings and the embed belong to this one agent. 
3. Add knowledge 
On the agent's page you can add knowledge several ways: 
Files : upload PDF, DOCX, TXT or CSV. 
Websites : add a URL to crawl, with scheduled re-crawls. 
Google Sheets (if enabled on your workspace): connect a published sheet that auto-syncs. 
Articles : author content directly. 
Knowledge is chunked, embedded and retrieved at answer time, with inline citations. 
4. Test & tune 
Use Test chat on the agent page to try it, and run reference Q/A sets under Evals to score accuracy, relevance and hallucination. 
5. Embed the widget 
Copy the snippet from your agent's Embed section and paste it before </body> . The widget is a self-contained Shadow-DOM bundle, so it won't clash with your site's styles. Restrict it to your sites under Allowed websites . 
6. Invite your team & route conversations 
Team & roles (Settings → Team): invite teammates as members (front-line staff) or admins. 
Inbox : your team picks up escalated conversations and replies, with canned responses a click away. 
Notifications : route escalations, SLA breaches and hot-lead alerts to Slack or email. Developer webhooks fire on conversation events (created / escalated / resolved, message created). 
Workspace administration 
Workspace-wide preferences (the workspace name, your team, notifications, language and your own profile) live under Settings .
