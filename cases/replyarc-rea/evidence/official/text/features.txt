Common questions 
Questions we get asked. 

How does it avoid making things up? 
It answers from your own content and nothing else: your website, and the PDFs, price lists and FAQs you give it. Every reply shows where it came from. When your content does not cover the question, it says so, gives your fallback answer, and asks for a way to follow up rather than guessing. 

Will it bother my visitors? 
It is proactive without being pushy. It opens at most once per page per visit, only on the pages you mark, and it stays quiet while someone is typing. If a visitor dismisses it, it leaves them alone for the rest of the visit. You choose how hard it pushes, from letting them lead to guiding them to the next step. 

What does it learn from? 
Paste your website URL and it reads your pages one by one. Drag in the PDFs, price lists and FAQs that hold the rest. No formatting, no cleanup: if a document has text in it, the agent can answer from it. You can add a page or a file any time something changes. 

Do I need a developer? 
No. You paste one script tag into HTML, Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Webflow or React. The widget renders in its own Shadow DOM, so your styles never break it and it never breaks yours. If you have not picked a colour, it samples your page and matches your brand. 

What happens when someone needs a person? 
Your team takes over from the inbox in one click, and the visitor sees one continuous thread rather than a restart. The agent only offers a human when someone is actually online. When you are away it takes contact details instead of queueing people into silence. 

What happens outside my opening hours? 
It says so honestly, sets an expectation for when you will reply, keeps answering from your content, and asks for an email so you can follow up. A question at 2am becomes a lead in your inbox instead of a visitor who left. 

Which languages does it speak? 
Six: English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Tamil and Thai. It detects the language message by message, so a visitor can switch mid-conversation and the agent follows. 

Does it replace WhatsApp? 
No, it hands visitors to it. Plenty of customers would rather carry on in the app they already use, so the widget carries one-tap shortcuts to WhatsApp, Messenger, LINE and WeChat, a button that dials your number, and a link to your booking page. Shortcuts appear only for the channels you switch on. 

Do I bring my own AI keys? 
No. We run the models, so there is nothing to configure and nothing to top up. A proprietary engagement algorithm and our own AI stack decide when to speak up, what to suggest, and when to stay quiet. 

How is it priced? 
By conversation, not by seat and not by message. The free plan covers 50 conversations a month and needs no card. 

How do I know it is working? 
Analytics show what the agent handled on its own, what reached a human, and how visitors rated the conversations. The most useful part is the list of questions it could not answer, because that is your next page to write.
