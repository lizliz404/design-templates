What happens when you paste 
A working agent, before you finish reading this 
No installation, no account, and nothing to undo afterwards. The demo is the real product, running on your own content. 

First 3 seconds 
We open your homepage 
A plain request to your public homepage, the same one a search engine makes. Nothing behind a login, no forms filled in, no customer data touched. 

Next 5 seconds 
It works out what you sell 
Headings, prices, hours and contact details come out as passages. It works out the questions your visitors are most likely to ask, and picks up the tone your own pages are written in. 

By second 10 
The agent is docked and answering 
Your brand colours come off the page itself, and the agent appears on a preview of your site in the corner your real widget would sit. It keeps reading the rest of your pages while you chat to it. 

Once it is built 
It is not a video. You get to drive it. 
The demo is the live widget with the training wheels off: everything below is something you can do to it in the first two minutes, with no account and no limit on how much you ask. 

01 Ask it the awkward ones 
Not the opening hours. Ask what it costs, what is in stock, whether you deliver to their postcode. Every answer names the page it came from, so you can check its working in one click. 

02 Try to catch it out 
Ask it something your site does not say. It tells you it does not know and offers a person, rather than inventing an answer. That behaviour is the product, and it is the fastest thing to verify. 

03 Flip it between three products 
A switch in the demo turns the corner widget into a plain launcher, then a scripted chatbot, then the AI agent. The difference between the three is the whole argument, and you get to run it yourself. 

04 Let it speak first 
Turn on AktivReply™ and watch the agent open the conversation instead of waiting in the corner. On your live site it picks its moment, at most once a visit, and stays quiet while somebody is typing. 

05 Change its personality 
Five tone presets: friendly, professional, playful, concise, luxury. Tap one and the greeting repaints in front of you, which is about how long it takes on the real thing. 

06 Put one of your products in it 
Type in a service and a price and watch the agent recommend it as a card in the chat. That is the selling half of the job, not just the answering half.

Do I have to sign up? No. No account, no card, no email address. Paste the address and the demo builds. You only make an account if you decide to keep the agent. 
What exactly do you read on my site? Public pages, starting at your homepage and following the links it can reach, which is the same access a search engine has. Nothing behind a login, no forms are submitted, and no customer data is touched. 
How long does it take? About ten seconds to get your homepage in and the agent answering. It keeps reading the rest of your pages in the background while you are chatting to it, so the answers get better as you go. 
How long does the demo stay up? About a day. Save it with your email and it stays for a week. Claim it free and it stops being a demo: the agent moves into your account already trained, and you carry on from there. 
Will you email me? Only if you ask us to save the demo. That is the one thing that puts an address on the record; the rest of the demo runs without one. 
What if my site is small, or built in JavaScript? If we cannot frame your site we draw a clean stand-in and dock the agent on that. The answers still come from your pages either way. A small site means fewer questions it can answer, which is exactly what the unanswered-questions list in the dashboard is for: it tells you the next page worth writing. 
Is the demo the real product, or a mock-up? The real widget and the real engine. Two differences: it is trained on your homepage rather than your whole site, and the source citations are left visible so you can check its working. On a live site only you see those, in the dashboard. 
What does it cost after the demo? There is a free plan with no card, and the AI is included on every plan, so there is no model bill and no API key to bring. The bar at the top of this page carries the current allowance.
