FAQ 
The questions we get asked most 
Still unsure? Email [email protected] and a human answers. 

Will it make things up about my business? 
No. It answers only from what it has read on your website and the documents you gave it, and every reply cites the page it came from. Asked something it has no grounding for, it says so and offers a human rather than guessing at a price or a policy. 

Will it annoy my visitors? 
Most people do not like AI agents, and that is exactly why this one is built to stay quiet. It only speaks first on the pages you mark, in the posture you choose, after a delay you set, and it holds back from anyone who is mid-read. Everywhere else it waits to be opened. 

Can my team take over a conversation? 
At any point. Reply as yourself and the agent steps back for that thread, with no repetition for the visitor. Threads that need a person sit in their own lane so they never get lost behind the ones already resolved. 

Do I need my own AI provider account? 
No. The models, the embeddings and the retrieval infrastructure are all included in your plan. There are no provider keys to manage and no separate model bill. You bring your content, we handle the rest. 

How is a conversation counted? 
One genuine back and forth between a visitor and your agent. Bots, crawlers, gibberish and your own testing never count, and a page refresh is the same conversation counted once. If junk slips through, mark it as spam from your inbox and it is credited back. 

How long does setup actually take? 
About ten minutes. Paste your URL and the agent reads your site, spend five minutes checking what it learned, then paste one script tag or install the WordPress plugin. No rebuild, and no developer needed for the standard install. 

How good is it in languages other than English? 
All six languages read the same knowledge base, so the facts are identical whichever one a visitor uses. English, Chinese, Malay, Tamil, Thai and Indonesian, detected automatically from the visitor.
