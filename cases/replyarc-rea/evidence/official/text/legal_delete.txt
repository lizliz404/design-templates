Delete your account and data 
Last updated 24 June 2026 

You can delete your ReplyArc account, and the data associated with it, at any time. This page explains how to do it, what is removed, and how long it takes. It covers both the ReplyArc web dashboard at app.replyarc.ai and the ReplyArc mobile app (iOS and Android). ReplyArc is operated by Wondertabs Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202037320G, Singapore). 
1. How to delete your account 
In the mobile app 
Open the ReplyArc app and sign in. 
Go to Settings . 
Tap Delete account , then confirm. 
On the web, or if you cannot sign in 
Email [email protected] from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify that you own the account and complete the deletion for you. 
2. What gets deleted 
If you are the sole owner of a workspace , deleting your account removes the entire workspace and everything in it, including: 
your account and sign-in credentials; 
your bots, their knowledge base, and settings; 
conversations, messages, and transcripts; 
captured leads and visitor records; 
other team members' access to that workspace; and 
your billing subscription, which is cancelled immediately. 
If you are a member of a workspace owned by someone else (or one of several owners), deleting your account removes your personal access and sign-in. Content that belongs to the shared workspace, such as conversations handled by the wider team, stays with the workspace so the business can keep operating. A remaining owner can delete the workspace itself using the same steps. 
3. Push notifications 
If you used the mobile app, the device push token tied to your account is removed when you delete your account, and also whenever you sign out. 
4. Timing and retention 
Your access ends immediately. 
Your personal data is removed from our live systems immediately, and from encrypted backups within 30 days . 
We keep a limited set of records only where the law requires it, for example billing and tax records (invoices) held by our payment processor, Stripe. These are retained for the legally required period and then deleted, and are not used for any other purpose. 
5. Questions 
Email [email protected] . For the full detail on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy .
